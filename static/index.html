📞 Stage 1 — Intake Chat

The member types (or speaks) their problem. The LLM doesn't just chat — it extracts structured JSON: problem type, location, vehicle, and a safety check. No transcript, just data.

💡 The output of this stage is JSON, not a conversation. That's what makes it usable downstream — triage rules run on structured fields, not free text.

Why not let the AI triage?
⚠️ For safety-critical calls, hard-coded rules only. An LLM could hallucinate a highway breakdown as "can wait." Accident / highway / injury = always top of queue, zero AI judgment.

⚡ Stage 2 — Triage Queue

The AI classifies the call. Deterministic logic orders the queue. The AI never decides priority on safety-critical jobs.

💡 AI role: classify problem type. Rules role: set priority. These are two different things and must stay separate.

🚐 Stage 3 — Dispatch Board

This is the heavy lift — and it's not an AI problem. It's logistics software: driver app, job queue, assignment algorithm, GPS tracking, and a state machine.

💡 This is where most of the engineering months go. Either build it or integrate an existing roadside dispatch SaaS. The AI pipeline wraps around this — it doesn't replace it.

💬 Stage 4 — Status Chat

Member asks "where's my driver?" — the AI does a database lookup and translates raw status data into a friendly reply. It's a lookup wrapped in nice wording, not magic.

💡 The AI's role here is translation, not intelligence. It takes "status: en_route, eta: 12min, dist: 4.1mi" and makes it feel human. The data is always from the database — never hallucinated.

📊 Stage 5 — Survey + Improvement Loop

Post-service rating + transcript stored for every job. The improvement loop is human-driven prompt iteration, not autonomous self-training.

💡 "The AI trains itself" doesn't work at this scale. What actually improves it: log everything, read the 1–2 star transcripts, fix your prompts and routing rules, build an eval set. Maybe fine-tune way down the line.

⚠️ "The AI trains itself from survey scores" — this is the one part that doesn't work how most people picture. A small operator does not retrain an LLM from ratings. Too expensive, needs ML expertise, wrong tool for the problem.
